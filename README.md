# milan_game
for milan game jam 2026 Raman hostel

## game concept 
theme was time is money. so thought about a story based game where the player explicit goal is to get a set amount of time.but while doing so they neglect their mental health,which leads to problems later on. Wanted the player to challenge the idea of "time is money" meaning they shouldn't convert all their time into getting money.

## core gameplay loop

The players takes the instructions given at face value and picks options related to money making.but the responses or those actions would silently degrade mental health of player and they would get really negetive dialogues.

## intended player experience 

something like undertale. where on first playthrough you get the bad ending but in the second playthrough you explore other options and find out this game is not about only making money.

## how theme is incorporated
time is money. can imply that time is just as important as money.wanted to portray that it is more important than money. and the theme or limited time to earn money,and literally converting time into money.

## design decisions
hiding and tracking mental health related stats. To make it more realistic.like in real life we often ignore it and keep it hidden without realizing how much of a impact it has on our life. wanted to make A story based game a challenge and to try something new.

## cuts and incomplete ideas.
it was supposed to be a low poly 3d game which happens in just one room(Work from home instead of going to work). like the game
"static speaks to me" and many more events were supposed to be there like going out with friends, and was gonna start with much worse mental health, going towards suicidal. these were due time constraints.
